## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: React 19 + Vite, GSAP ScrollTrigger for scroll choreography, a single hand-written Canvas 2D layer for the system topology, and native CSS sticky and discrete story beats, SVG system stories, and an event-driven requestAnimationFrame loop scrubbing an H.264 pose clip with an optional WebGL portrait stage for the living portrait (no Framer, no Motion). Chosen because the site is a single continuous narrative with no server needs; Next.js would add SSR complexity with no SEO route benefit for one page. Static deploy (Vercel-compatible).

## Product Purpose

A personal brand experience for Prawal Sharma, Technology Manager at Successive Digital. It must communicate a progression from writing software to owning systems, teams, platforms and AI-driven engineering capability, and make résumé, experience, work, recognition and contact findable within seconds.

## Positioning

Ten years inside one company (Successive Digital, since 2017) rising from trainee to Technology Manager, with verifiable systems at scale: a 200+ source media data platform, an ACH payment platform handling 160K+ daily transactions at 99.9% uptime, and a measured 20-30% AI-assisted productivity lift across teams. Calm, zero-escalation delivery across US, UK and South Africa.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Two-layer single-page narrative: concise cinematic scenes above complete professional detail in shared native dialogs. Chapter anchors, manual scene selectors and detail triggers remain keyboard reachable. Closing a detail view preserves the visitor’s place and returns focus.
- No autoplaying audio. The page never depends on video: the living portrait is a desktop enhancement over an identical still frame, and every fallback (touch, phone, reduced motion, decode failure) is that still.
- Must not depend at runtime on the old portfolio. Old portfolio is read-only.
- Client work is under NDA: no client screenshots; architecture visuals are abstractions, never claimed diagrams.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Prawal Sharma. Identity fidelity over stylization. The owner approved (2026-09-25) a generated charcoal-wardrobe head-turn video for the opening's living portrait, chosen for closest likeness among the generations and judged against his real photographs; the close keeps the real photograph. Never crossfade two generated takes of him, and never ship a generation that reduces likeness.
- Cinematic, restrained, mature, technical. Not SaaS, cyberpunk, crypto, gamer, or template.

## Product Principles

1. Proof over adjectives: every claim traces to the source data.
2. The person is present: Prawal leads the opening as a living portrait, and the real photograph anchors the close.
3. Cinematic but navigable: the story never blocks access to facts.
4. Restraint signals seniority.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic headings, and a reduced-motion mode that is a designed composition rather than a disabled one.
